Worcestershire’s BEAS grants enter final phase; SMEs urged to claim energy support

March 25, 2026

Worcestershire County Council published its 2026–28 plan confirming that BEAS-backed SME energy grants are nearing their final payments before the scheme ends in March 2026. The council says over 250 local SMEs have received energy reviews and more than £1 million in grants so far, cutting over 500 tonnes of CO₂ per year. It also notes up to another £1 million is expected to be paid out before the deadline, with grants of up to £100,000 available after an audit. SME owners should book an energy review now and submit grant applications quickly to secure funding before BEAS closes.

Scottish funding update spotlights UK grant for SME workplace EV charging

March 24, 2026

Volunteer Centre East Lothian’s March funding roundup highlights the UK Government’s Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Grant for Staff and Fleets, aimed at SMEs. The grant helps small businesses pay for EV chargepoints and supporting infrastructure installed at their own premises. It can sit alongside other incentives, helping firms cut fuel costs and reduce Scope 1–2 emissions. SME owners should check eligibility and apply through the government’s “Find a Grant” portal linked in the update.
