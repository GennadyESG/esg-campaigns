Vietnam launches SME ESG handbook initiative to cut reporting costs and complexity

April 13, 2026

ACCA, the Vietnam Association of Small and Medium Enterprises (VINASME) and KPMG have kicked off work on a practical ESG handbook for small businesses in Vietnam. The guide aims to translate sustainability into simple steps for SMEs, including templates for data collection and reporting. Organisers say the handbook is designed to reduce cost and complexity while helping firms meet buyer and lender ESG requests. VINASME will circulate the tool nationwide, and SMEs are encouraged to join consultations and prepare basic emissions, social and governance data now.

EU platform proposes SME Sustainable Finance Standard to unlock greener bank lending

April 13, 2026

The EU Platform on Sustainable Finance has outlined an SME Sustainable Finance Standard to simplify how banks classify and report green loans to small and medium businesses. The proposal streamlines Taxonomy‑linked criteria, aiming to cut duplicate ESG questionnaires and make climate‑related financing more accessible to SMEs. The update also flags EFRAG’s April engagement on voluntary sustainability reporting activities, with stakeholder inputs due by April 20, 2026. SMEs can prepare by mapping project data (energy use, emissions cuts, adaptation benefits) to lender‑ready metrics and discussing eligibility with their banks.

Ireland promotes VSME briefings to help SMEs meet value‑chain sustainability demands

April 17, 2026

Chartered Accountants Ireland is highlighting IBEC’s briefing on the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for SMEs (VSME) to help smaller firms respond to customer and lender ESG requests. The session covers core disclosures, proportional reporting, and how SMEs can use VSME to structure low‑burden sustainability reporting. It targets owners, finance leads and compliance managers who need a simple, standardised approach to ESG data. SMEs can register for the April briefing and begin aligning internal data collection to VSME’s practical requirements.
