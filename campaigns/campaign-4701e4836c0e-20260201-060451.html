World Bank unlocks KSh 5.5bn green fund to finance Kenyan SME transitions

January 19, 2026

The World Bank approved about KSh 5.55 billion (US$43 million) for Kenya’s Green Investment Fund, targeted at small and medium‑sized enterprises adopting climate‑friendly solutions. Financing will support sectors like e‑mobility, energy‑efficient and green buildings, sustainable agriculture, and waste management. The fund will be managed independently and prioritise inclusion, with a focus on women‑owned SMEs. SME owners can prepare bankable proposals now and monitor Kenya Development Corporation announcements to access concessional ESG financing.

Malaysia’s new incentive framework ties tax breaks to sustainability, lifting SME ESG

January 30, 2026

Malaysia’s investment agency MIDA confirmed a New Incentive Framework effective March 1 that links tax incentives to measured outcomes such as sustainability under the National Investment Aspirations scorecard. Manufacturing moves first, with services due in Q2 2026, while existing approvals remain unaffected. The SME Association of Malaysia says the framework should deepen domestic linkages and push SMEs to upgrade technology and ESG standards to win supplier roles. SME suppliers can act now by mapping their data to the scorecard, documenting ESG practices, and pursuing recognised certifications needed by large buyers.

EU energy week extends SME efficiency awards deadline; apply with scalable ESG models

January 31, 2026

The European Sustainable Energy Week extended the “SMEs Driving Energy Efficiency” award deadline to February 5, 2026. The category recognises small and medium businesses that deploy innovative business or financing models for energy upgrades in buildings and industry. Submissions are judged on impact, innovation, scalability and how projects enable affordable energy savings for customers. SME owners with proven pilots or early roll‑outs should apply now, showing verified savings data, customer uptake and a plan to scale.
