Penang launches RM100m climate fund to help manufacturing SMEs meet ESG goals

January 6, 2026

Alliance Bank and the Penang Green Council created a RM100 million Climate Mitigation Fund for Penang-based manufacturing SMEs and mid-tier companies. The fund uses Bank Negara’s climate taxonomy to label projects and offers simplified assessments, ring‑fenced use of proceeds, and workshops on ESG adoption. Preferential rates and a soft cap of RM5 million per borrower aim to speed investments in solar, efficient equipment, circular waste, and other upgrades. The program helps SMEs meet growing buyer requirements and local energy targets while cutting operating costs.

Malaysia SME group demands incentives as buyers push ESG data and reporting

January 5, 2026

Malaysia’s SME association warned that small firms risk being squeezed by a surge in compliance demands, including buyer requests for emissions and ESG data. It called for a shift from strict enforcement to incentive‑led support so SMEs can remain in supply chains. Proposals include an SME Transition Academy to train sustainability officers and a national fund to help automate ESG reporting. SME owners should expect more ESG questionnaires from large customers and plan budget and capacity accordingly.

EU deforestation rules revised; small operators get simplified declarations and delay

January 8, 2026

A January legal update confirms targeted changes to the EU Deforestation Regulation that affect SMEs in wood, rubber, coffee, cocoa and other chains. All operators must comply by December 30, 2026, while micro and small operators have until June 30, 2027 and can file a one‑off simplified declaration. Due‑diligence responsibility shifts to the first operator placing goods on the EU market, reducing burdens for downstream SMEs. Smaller importers and suppliers should map affected lines, update contracts, and prepare traceability data ahead of the new dates.

HKPC hosts practical ESG and climate risk workshop tailored for Hong Kong SMEs

January 14, 2026

Hong Kong Productivity Council will run “Building Resilient SMEs: Combining Climate Data and ESG for Sustainable Finance.” The session covers how SMEs can assess climate risks, improve ESG reporting, and unlock green financing. Organisers promise practical guidance suited to SME resources and lender expectations. Owners can register to gain checklists and approaches that are usable immediately in their businesses.

Ireland unveils 2026–2030 capital plan with €300m for SME decarbonisation

January 7, 2026

Ireland’s Sectoral Capital Plan 2026–2030 commits €300 million to enterprise decarbonisation alongside broader SME supports. The plan also funds local enterprise services and scaling programs to help indigenous firms grow and modernise. Decarbonisation funding will back low‑carbon operations and efficiency investments that cut energy costs and emissions. SMEs should watch for calls from Enterprise Ireland and SBCI lenders as schemes roll out.
