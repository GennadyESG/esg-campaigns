KOTRA runs one-day workshop to help export SMEs meet new ESG disclosures

November 27, 2025

KOTRA hosted a nationwide workshop on ESG disclosure specifically for export SMEs. The session focused on how small exporters can respond to 2025 buyer and regulatory ESG information requests, with practical guidance and templates. Registration ran November 14–25, and the program targeted immediate readiness for disclosure demands from global customers.

Why it matters for SMEs: It gives exporting SMEs a step‑by‑step plan to satisfy ESG data requests and avoid losing orders due to non‑compliance.

Japan’s METI hosts SME decarbonization workshop in Miyazaki with hands‑on case studies

November 26, 2025

The Kyushu Bureau of METI held a free, in‑person workshop to help SMEs and their advisers advance energy‑saving and decarbonization. Officials briefed attendees on the latest policy and then ran group exercises using a model SME to identify actions, stakeholder buy‑in, and next steps. The training targeted SME advisors, banks, local officials, and in‑house SME managers responsible for energy and emissions.

Why it matters for SMEs: Firms can copy the worksheets and workflow to cut energy costs now and prepare evidence for customer ESG reporting.

KLSCCCI’s SME summit session guides SMEs on aligning capital with ESG

November 28, 2025

The Kuala Lumpur and Selangor Chinese Chamber of Commerce and Industry hosted “Investing for Impact: Aligning Capital with ESG” as part of its SME Business Summit. The session explained how Malaysian SMEs can structure funding strategies to meet investor and lender ESG expectations. It sits within a broader summit program aimed at SME leaders, including ESG‑focused forums and briefings.

Why it matters for SMEs: It shows concrete steps to secure cheaper, ESG‑aligned financing and signal credibility to banks and investors.
