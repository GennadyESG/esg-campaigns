CIMB launches GHG advisory to help Malaysian SMEs meet stricter ESG demands

June 22, 2026

CIMB introduced a Greenhouse Gas Advisory Service under its GreenBizReady proposition to help SMEs and mid-tier companies measure emissions and plan decarbonisation. The bank says this support will prepare suppliers for Malaysia’s new climate disclosure rules and buyer requests, including Scope 3 data. It also references a Companies Commission consultative paper proposing mandatory Scope 1 and 2 reporting for certain non‑listed firms, signaling tighter expectations ahead. SME owners can engage the advisory to get a baseline, build targets, and unlock sustainability‑linked financing.

Reset Connect London hub gives SMEs practical supply chain decarbonisation playbooks

June 23, 2026

At London Climate Action Week, the Business & Supply Chain Hub gathered sustainability leaders to translate net‑zero goals into concrete SME actions. Sessions focused on financing options, emissions tracking, supplier engagement and preparing for evolving reporting requirements. The agenda featured SME Climate Hub leadership and buyers, offering small businesses a way to align with customer ESG expectations and win tenders. Owners can use these toolkits and clinics to build roadmaps, cut energy costs and improve data for buyer questionnaires.
