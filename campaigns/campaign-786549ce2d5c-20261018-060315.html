Malaysia funds ESG supply-chain coaching for SMEs; seats limited, register now

October 12, 2026

Malaysia’s chamber network is inviting SMEs to a four‑day ESG Supply Chain Training & Coaching Programme this month. The course is 90% funded by SME Corp. Malaysia, with UN Global Compact Network Malaysia & Brunei as organiser and ACCCIM as anchor partner, and costs RM525 per participant. Topics include building a sustainability action plan, setting measurable ESG targets, materiality and reporting, ESG communications, and using the central bank’s CCPT framework to access green finance. Seats are capped and firms must hold a valid SME Status Certificate before Day 1, so owners should register quickly and prepare basic ESG data.

Free 30‑minute ESG starter session for SMEs during NSW Small Business Month

October 13, 2026

New South Wales is running a short, practical webinar titled “ESG Without the Overwhelm” for small businesses. The session explains why ESG matters for SMEs now (customer demands, supply chains, new expectations) and shows simple first steps to begin. Owners can join at no cost and use the guidance to prioritise quick wins like basic policies, supplier checks and simple emissions tracking. It is part of a three‑session series, so attendees can plan follow‑ups to deepen actions.

Poland’s SME agency flags new green tools, events and calls for MŚP

October 17, 2026

PARP’s ESG hub for SMEs lists immediate actions: an October 19 study visit on circular economy practices and upcoming applications for green and digital transformation support. It highlights loans for enterprise “green and digital” upgrades and an October 29 opening of the SMART path call for R&D projects. The page also links free online courses on sustainability, energy efficiency and circular economy designed specifically for MŚP. SME owners can register for the event, line up documents, and target the most relevant financing window for ESG improvements.
