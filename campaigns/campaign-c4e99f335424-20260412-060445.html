Malaysia bolsters SME energy transition with TNB incentives, Time‑of‑Use and solar finance

April 11, 2026

Malaysia’s Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives Ministry agreed with utility TNB to help MSMEs manage rising electricity costs. Plans include aligning incentives or tailored tariffs for SMEs and expanding the Time‑of‑Use scheme to cut bills. The partnership will also open flexible zero‑capex options for rooftop solar and add capability‑building via INSKEN, in line with national ESG goals. SME owners can review ToU eligibility, speak to TNB about tariff options, and monitor Kuskop/INSKEN channels for new solar financing programs.

OCBC targets 12,000 SMEs for sustainable financing across ASEAN by 2028

April 7, 2026

OCBC set a goal to support 12,000 SMEs in Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, and Indonesia with sustainable finance by 2028. Commitments are expected to reach about SGD 25 billion, with more sustainability‑linked loans and social loans for women entrepreneurs. The bank will scale tools like the SME Energy Efficiency Assessment and the Start‑ESG programme with Enterprise Singapore to help firms measure baselines and access funding. SMEs can contact OCBC to assess eligibility, prepare utility and operations data for KPIs, and use Start‑ESG assessments to speed approvals.

Vietnam launches six‑month ESG accelerator to help SMEs meet reporting standards fast

April 8, 2026

The Ministry of Finance’s APED and GRI, backed by Switzerland’s SECO, introduced the S.T.A.R. Accelerator for Vietnamese SMEs. The program will select 30–50 firms to build ESG governance, collect baseline data, and prepare simplified sustainability reports in six months. CRIF’s digital platform and expert mentoring will standardize questionnaires, scoring, and improvement plans tied to lender and buyer expectations. SMEs should register interest, appoint an internal lead, and prepare basic emissions, energy, and workforce data to qualify.

EIB proposes blended loan with Intesa to spur Albania’s green SME upgrades

April 7, 2026

The European Investment Bank published a new facility with Intesa Sanpaolo Bank Albania to finance innovative and green SME and mid‑cap projects. The EUR 22 million pipeline aims to boost investments in digitalization, climate action, and environmental sustainability. Final beneficiaries must comply with national law and EIB environmental and social standards; the project is under appraisal before approval. Albanian SMEs can approach Intesa branches now to discuss eligibility for energy‑efficiency upgrades, renewables, and digital projects.

osapiens unveils EASY START suite to simplify EU ESG compliance for SMEs

April 8, 2026

osapiens launched EASY START, a preconfigured toolset for small and mid‑sized companies facing EU rules such as CSRD, EUDR, and packaging laws. The suite includes VSME/GRI‑aligned templates, a Scope 1–3 calculator, EUDR traceability, and automated reporting outputs like XBRL. It positions reliable ESG data as a way to lower compliance costs and improve access to finance and supply chains. SMEs can onboard quickly, centralize supplier documentation, and generate standard reports demanded by lenders and large buyers.
