HSBC offers European SMEs loans with pricing tied to third‑party ESG scores

October 7, 2026

HSBC launched a Sustainability Improvement Loan linking interest rates to a small or mid‑market borrower’s ESG score. Pricing moves up or down based on ratings from providers like EcoVadis, CDP or Inrate, avoiding the complexity of bespoke KPI‑linked structures. The product is now rolling out across continental Europe after prior launches in the UK, Asia and the Middle East. SMEs can act by securing a third‑party ESG rating and applying to benefit from preferential pricing.

EU voluntary reporting standard limits CSRD data requests on SMEs; CIF explains actions

October 5, 2026

Ireland’s Construction Industry Federation outlined how the EU’s new Voluntary Standard gives companies outside CSRD scope a proportionate dataset to answer sustainability requests. The article notes that Omnibus I raised CSRD thresholds to over 1,000 employees and €450 million turnover, moving many firms out of mandatory reporting. It also explains the “value‑chain cap” from FY2027: CSRD reporters should not ask suppliers with ≤1,000 employees for information beyond the Voluntary Standard for their own CSRD disclosures. SMEs can adopt the Voluntary Standard now to cut duplicate questionnaires and satisfy client and lender requests.

EU Innovation Fund announces SME‑only call; webinar for applicants on October 13

October 13, 2026

The Commission’s new IF26 SME Call is designed specifically for small and medium enterprises with smaller clean‑tech projects. It offers a simpler application, a semi‑automated GHG‑avoidance calculator, lighter reporting and two annual cut‑off dates, with an indicative €200 million budget. Projects with capex between €2.5–€20 million qualify, and SME‑only consortia are allowed. SMEs can join the October 13 webinar to learn eligibility, timelines and how to apply.

Indonesia’s BSI scales ESG financing; thousands of UMKM advance into larger segments

October 9, 2026

Bank Syariah Indonesia reported Rp78.6 trillion in ESG‑aligned financing as of June 2026, equal to 23.1% of its portfolio, up 7.09% year‑on‑year. The bank highlighted upward mobility among SME borrowers: around 17,000 micro KUR clients moved into small and commercial segments, about 4,000 small KUR advanced to commercial, and roughly 1,000 graduated from micro to SME. BSI frames its approach around three ESG pillars and positions green lending as a growth enabler for smaller firms. Indonesian SMEs can approach BSI for sustainability‑linked financing to upgrade operations and access larger markets.
