EU adopts revised ESRS and voluntary SME standard with value chain cap

July 3, 2026

The European Commission adopted updated sustainability reporting standards and a new voluntary reporting standard for smaller companies. The revision cuts mandatory datapoints by over 60% and is expected to lower reporting costs by more than 30% per company. A “value chain cap” limits what large customers can ask SMEs to provide, making information requests more manageable. SMEs can start using the voluntary standard now while the package goes through a two‑month scrutiny period in Parliament and Council.

Netherlands extends SVOM subsidy to help SMEs implement supply chain due diligence

July 1, 2026

The SVOM grant supports Dutch SMEs to apply OECD due‑diligence steps on human rights and environmental risks. Firms can apply for €10,000 (minimum project cost €20,000) on a first‑come, first‑served basis until November 3, 2026. Eligible activities include risk analysis in the supply chain, stakeholder dialogue, policy development, and complaint mechanisms. This funding helps SMEs prepare for indirect requirements from EU laws like CSRD and CSDDD.

Netherlands consults on SME energy-saving loans up to €250,000 per location

June 30, 2026

The government opened a public consultation on loans for SMEs to finance energy‑saving measures. Target firms include those under the energy‑saving duty and smaller users with at least 10,000 kWh electricity or 5,000 m³ gas annually. Proposed loans range from €10,000 to €250,000 per location, with up to seven‑year terms, covering specified measures like audits, upgrades, and building adjustments. SMEs can submit feedback until July 28, 2026, and prepare to apply during the planned October 1–December 15, 2026 window.

Philippines’ PTTC joins Net Zero Carbon Alliance to train and support MSMEs

July 3, 2026

The Philippine Trade Training Center signed an MOU with the Net Zero Carbon Alliance to boost decarbonization support for MSMEs. PTTC becomes the alliance’s first public‑sector member, signaling stronger government–private cooperation on climate action. The partnership focuses on capacity building, knowledge sharing, and tools that help small firms start practical net‑zero plans. MSMEs can connect with PTTC programs at the MSME Hub to access training and guidance.
