EU releases updated SME sustainability reporting template and XBRL tools for VS compliance

November 4, 2026

EFRAG published Version 2.0.0 of the Voluntary Standard (VS) Digital Template, with an updated XBRL taxonomy and converter for SMEs. The tools let small and medium companies prepare sustainability data and export Inline XBRL reports that lenders and big customers can read easily. Source code is open‑source (MIT), and the converter includes full XBRL validation to reduce reporting errors. SMEs up to 1,000 employees can use the template now to respond to value‑chain data requests and prepare simple, structured ESG reports.

EFRAG’s VSME/VS template 2.0.0 released; SMEs urged to stop using old files

November 4, 2026

A regulatory watch update confirms EFRAG’s new VS Digital Template 2.0.0 and asks users to replace earlier versions, which are now superseded. The note links the update to the EU delegated act adopting the voluntary SME standard and reminds companies that a “value‑chain cap” limits data asked from SMEs. It also flags that this cap applies for financial years starting January 1, 2027, so SMEs should migrate templates now. Practical takeaway: download version 2.0.0 and restart draft reports in the new file to avoid conversion problems later.

Singapore’s Gprnt Green 100 offers free starter ESG reporting for local SMEs

November 5, 2026

A Gprnt‑led survey of 560 SMEs found only 25% have started any sustainability efforts, so the platform is steering firms to its free, simple tracking under the Green 100 program. SMEs can log basic energy and water data and receive a recognition badge from the Singapore Environment Council, helping with green procurement and financing. The initiative works through “queen bee” corporates that invite their SME suppliers to join, lowering onboarding effort. For owners, the next step is to register on Gprnt and begin basic Scope 1 and 2 tracking to meet growing customer data requests.
