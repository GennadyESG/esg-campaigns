EU opens Innovation Fund Info Days, adds new SME-dedicated clean tech grants

November 17, 2026

The European Commission announced five Innovation Fund calls opening on December 3, including a brand-new grant line dedicated to SMEs. Two SME application rounds are planned for end‑March and end‑September 2027, with a total fund budget of up to €5.2 billion across all calls. Four livestreamed Info Days in December will explain eligibility, funding conditions, evaluation and how to apply. SME owners should register, review the SME call page, and line up project data and partners now.

First EU Innovation Fund call reserved for SMEs details budgets, simpler process

November 19, 2026

An official EU platform confirmed a €200 million Innovation Fund call exclusively for SMEs, opening December 3. Projects with €2.5–€20 million in capital expenditure can apply, with streamlined documentation, simpler reporting, and a tool to calculate greenhouse‑gas savings. SMEs have two deadlines in 2027 and can book 25‑minute orientation dialogues with the Commission and CINEA. Owners in manufacturing and clean tech should prepare proposals and register for the December 10 SME Info Day.

MUFG, ESGpedia, Hitachi partner to link SME ESG data with decarbonization finance

November 19, 2026

A new APAC memorandum of understanding aims to connect ESG reporting, building‑level analytics, and financing to help businesses decarbonize. The partners plan an integrated pathway from emissions measurement and reporting to implementing and funding energy‑efficiency upgrades. Initial focus markets include Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, and Indonesia. SMEs in the building value chain should monitor pilot offerings and prepare facility data to access future financing options.
