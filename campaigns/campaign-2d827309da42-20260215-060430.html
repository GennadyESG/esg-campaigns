EIB expands EU-wide SME energy efficiency financing, adds servitisation to scale adoption

February 11, 2026

The European Investment Bank Group reported that its Energy Efficiency for SMEs initiative delivered €6 billion of financing in 2025, enabling up to 150,000 small firms to upgrade and decarbonise. Between 2025 and 2027, the programme targets €17.5 billion in financing to support as many as 350,000 SMEs to cut energy costs and emissions. New partnerships with Eiffel Investment Group and Solas Capital will pilot “energy efficiency as a service,” shifting upfront equipment costs from SMEs to service providers. The initiative, part of the EIB Group’s 2024–2027 Strategic Roadmap, is on track to surpass its original goals.

North Macedonia launches €25m green grants to help SMEs cut emissions

February 12, 2026

North Macedonia’s innovation agency INOVA announced a €25 million green support programme for 2026–2030 to help SMEs invest in environmental protection and sustainability. The scheme allocates €22 million in direct subsidies, with grants from €5,000 to €300,000 and co‑financing requirements of 20%–50%, depending on project type. The EU will contribute €18 million, and at least 300 companies—mainly micro, small and medium manufacturers—are expected to benefit through equipment purchases, technology adoption and advisory support. The first public call is planned around mid‑2026, with separate tracks for green startups, modernisation and industrial system transformation.

Singapore Budget 2026 extends SME Energy Efficiency Grant and green loans

February 12, 2026

Singapore will extend the Energy Efficiency Grant to March 31, 2027 to co‑fund companies’ investments in pre‑approved energy‑efficient equipment. SMEs can receive up to 70% support (30% for non‑SMEs), with eligible sectors including manufacturing, food services, retail, construction, data centres and maritime. Support for green loans under the Enterprise Financing Scheme will also be extended to March 31, 2031, improving access to financing for firms developing or adopting green solutions. The measures aim to help businesses invest in sustainable technologies while managing cost pressures, said Finance Minister Lawrence Wong.
