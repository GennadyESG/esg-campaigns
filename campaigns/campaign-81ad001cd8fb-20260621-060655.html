Kyrgyzstan signs $59m green guarantees to unlock SME clean energy projects

June 15, 2026

The World Bank approved $59 million for a Green Guarantees component to expand sustainable finance in Kyrgyzstan. The Kyrgyz Guarantee Fund will implement the scheme, backing loans for entrepreneurs and SMEs investing in renewable energy, energy efficiency, industrial upgrades, and sustainable agriculture. Officials say the guarantee mechanism is designed to make green lending more accessible and lower-risk for smaller firms. SME owners can now prepare eligible projects and approach participating lenders with the new guarantee support in place.

ILO and VCCI launch electronics supplier program with ESG reporting training

June 17, 2026

The ILO and Vietnam’s Chamber of Commerce and Industry opened the Compliance Edge Lab 2026–27 for up to 50 electronics factories, many of them SMEs. The program offers needs-based onsite and offsite training that includes ESG reporting requirements, labor standards, and risk assessment. It targets suppliers under rising global due‑diligence and sustainability expectations, aiming to improve competitiveness and working conditions. Electronics SMEs can register and use the training to build practical systems for ESG data, compliance, and customer audits.

Tourism NI opens free sustainability programme to help SMEs cut costs

June 16, 2026

Tourism Northern Ireland is inviting expressions of interest for Cohort 6 of the Sustainable Tourism Business Programme. The fully funded offer (over £2,000 value) provides online and in‑person sessions, university‑certified accreditation, and expert mentoring. SMEs will learn to reduce energy, water, and waste costs and build a practical sustainability action plan to strengthen their market positioning. Tourism business owners and senior decision‑makers can apply after a short information webinar, with tools and templates provided to speed implementation.

Mass Save expands Main Streets initiative to fund small business energy upgrades

June 15, 2026

Massachusetts utilities are continuing the 2026 Main Streets initiative to bring energy‑efficiency services and incentives to more small businesses. The program targets local commercial corridors with tailored outreach, onsite assessments, and streamlined rebates for lighting, HVAC, and other upgrades. Organizers say the goal is to lower operating costs while reducing waste and emissions across communities. Eligible small businesses can sign up when the program team visits their area or contact Mass Save advisors to start a project now.
