Alliance Bank launches ESG playbook to help Malaysia’s manufacturing SMEs decarbonise quickly

February 2, 2026

Alliance Bank Malaysia launched a practical ESG Playbook designed for manufacturing SMEs to embed sustainability into daily operations. Built with Monash University Malaysia, Zurich Malaysia and partners like MPMA, Penang Green Council and UN Global Compact Network Malaysia & Brunei, it includes local SME case studies and step‑by‑step guidance. The playbook introduces eight ESG leadership archetypes to help firms assess maturity, pick priorities and plan actions. SMEs can download the guide and use it to unlock new opportunities, reduce risk and prepare for customer and regulator requirements.

Spain unveils €40m grant to help freelancers and micro‑SMEs buy electric vehicles

February 5, 2026

The Spanish government announced a new €40 million aid line to support self‑employed workers and very small businesses (under 10 employees) in purchasing or renting electrified vehicles. The grant provides up to €7,500 per vehicle, giving small firms a direct incentive to electrify fleets and cut operating emissions. Madrid also confirmed the second call of the Industrial Decarbonisation PERTE Line 1 (€330 million) opening on February 6, targeting cleaner production. SMEs can use these funds to lower fuel costs, meet buyer sustainability expectations and prepare for future low‑emission zones.
