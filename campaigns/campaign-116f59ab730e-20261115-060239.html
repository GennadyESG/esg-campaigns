Uzbekistan unlocks $720m climate finance program prioritising SMEs and green technologies

November 11, 2026

Uzbekistan joined the Transformation Pathways Initiative, a $720 million program approved by the Green Climate Fund in October 2026. The scheme will channel $187 million from GCF and $533 million in co‑financing into long‑term loans and direct investments via local banks, with technical help for project preparation. It explicitly prioritises industrial firms and small and medium businesses to adopt energy efficiency, renewables, circular economy measures, and other low‑carbon upgrades. SME owners should prepare bankable green projects (energy audits, equipment lists, simple payback) and speak with local lenders about accessing this window.

Uzbekistan pilots SME toolkits and bank lending under Business and Human Rights

November 10, 2026

UNDP and the National Human Rights Centre held Uzbekistan’s first National Dialogue on Business and Human Rights, followed by training from November 11–13. Organisers highlighted that SMEs make up 85% of active companies and introduced a two‑tier SME toolkit covering HR due diligence, safety, ESG, compliance, and supplier management. The programme also includes voluntary pilots with banks and state‑owned firms to test lending and implementation approaches. SME owners can join follow‑on sessions, use the toolkit to map risks, and engage their bank about pilot lending tied to ESG and human‑rights safeguards.

Epoch raises $4.25m to launch SME EUDR compliance platform using AI

November 9, 2026

London startup Epoch secured a $4.25 million seed round to automate EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) compliance for small and mid‑sized importers. Its tool aims to replace manual supplier questionnaires with satellite and geospatial data, helping SMEs prove deforestation‑free supply chains ahead of EUDR deadlines. This targets a major gap: many SMEs lack resources for traceability and due‑diligence statements demanded by EU buyers. SME importers of covered commodities should trial such tools now, organise supplier data, and align processes with the EU’s EUDR information system timelines.
