National Green 100 enlists big buyers, onboards SMEs with simplified ESG disclosures

May 21, 2026

Singapore’s Council for a Competitive Climate Transition launched the Green 100 movement to help SMEs start sustainability reporting and capture new business opportunities. The programme mobilises large “Queen Bee” enterprises to invite at least 100 SME suppliers each to submit a short set of environmental data through the Gprnt platform. SMEs that complete the disclosure will receive a Green 100 badge issued by the Singapore Environment Council, improving visibility for green procurement and financing. SME owners can register on the platform, compile basic energy and resource-use information, and ask anchor customers to recognise the badge in supplier onboarding.

Singapore launches TR 149:2026 standard, giving SMEs a practical sustainability pathway

May 22, 2026

Enterprise Singapore and partners introduced TR 149:2026, a new technical reference that guides organisations to progress towards environmental sustainability excellence. The standard provides a clear, step‑by‑step specification that companies can adopt now to assess current practices, set priorities and improve performance. It is designed to be usable by SMEs and underpins simplified disclosure initiatives such as Green 100. SME owners can map existing data (e.g., energy, water and fuel use) to TR 149, assign a single data owner, and reuse outputs in buyer questionnaires and green‑loan applications.

EU’s revised ESRS and new VSME limit buyer ESG requests to SMEs

May 21, 2026

The European Commission opened consultation on revised ESRS and a voluntary SME reporting standard (VSME). The draft ESRS simplify sustainability reporting for CSRD‑in‑scope companies, cutting mandatory datapoints by more than 60% and total datapoints by over 70%. Crucially for SMEs, the VSME defines “protected undertakings” (under 1,000 employees) and creates a “value‑chain cap,” so large customers cannot demand ESG information beyond the VSME template starting in financial year 2027. SMEs can adopt the VSME now to standardise responses, assert the cap when handling questionnaires, and submit feedback by June 3, 2026.
