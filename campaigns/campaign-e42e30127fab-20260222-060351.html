CIMB and SME Corp Malaysia launch free ESG tools and training for SMEs

February 16, 2026

CIMB Bank and CIMB Islamic onboarded SME Corp Malaysia into the GreenBizReady programme to help SMEs start and scale ESG adoption. The tie-up gives SMEs complimentary access to an MSME ESG Assessment for an instant readiness score and an ESG Quick Guide to kickstart reporting. CIMB also introduced an ESG Simplified Playbook for SMEs, while SME Corp will run workshops, training and networking for participating firms. Action: enrol in GreenBizReady to benchmark your ESG status, get practical guidance, and prepare a first sustainability report.

Hang Seng unveils HK$80b fund, SME green equipment financing assessment platform

February 18, 2026

Hang Seng Bank launched the HK$80 billion Sustainability Power Up Fund to provide green and sustainability‑linked financing for businesses, including SMEs. A new SME Green Equipment Financing Assessment platform with HKQAA helps smaller firms validate and document eligibility for green equipment loans, reducing certification friction. Facilities follow Green Loan, Social Loan and Sustainability‑Linked Loan Principles, with a reward campaign offering up to HK$10,000 for successful applicants until May 15, 2026. Action: use the assessment platform to verify your project and apply for preferential‑rate green financing.

Uzbekistan to develop national SME ESG framework with GIZ and ACT Global

February 18, 2026

Uzbekistan’s Ministry of Economy and Finance began work with GIZ and ACT Global on a practical ESG and responsible business conduct framework tailored to SMEs. The initiative kicked off with stakeholder workshops in Tashkent on February 17–18, engaging regulators, banks, energy‑efficiency bodies and business groups. The framework aims to improve SME competitiveness and access to finance by aligning disclosures with international standards and promoting resource and energy efficiency. Action: SMEs should watch for pilot opportunities and consultations, and start gathering basic emissions, workforce and governance data to be early‑ready.
