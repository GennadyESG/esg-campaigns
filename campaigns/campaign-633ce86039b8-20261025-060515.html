EIB and BBVA unlock €1.3bn for Spanish SMEs, over €200m for green projects

October 21, 2026

The EIB Group invested €259 million in two BBVA securitisations to mobilise about €1.3 billion in new financing for Spanish SMEs and mid‑caps. More than €200 million will be reserved for green or sustainability‑related investments, including energy efficiency and renewable projects. The funding targets firms across Spain, with a focus on cohesion regions where access to finance is harder. SMEs can approach BBVA to seek working‑capital or investment loans, including for eligible green upgrades.

EDF opens 25 business resilience grants for UK small firms, sustainability eligible

October 22, 2026

EDF Small Business launched a new funding round offering 25 grants of £1,000 to eligible UK small businesses. The money can support practical improvements like energy‑efficiency upgrades, software, equipment, or consultancy to build resilience and cut costs. Applications opened on October 20 and close at 11:59pm on November 29, 2026. Owners can review criteria and apply via the Powering Local Businesses programme page.

CIMB unveils new sustainability‑linked financing options for Malaysian SMEs and property

October 19, 2026

CIMB introduced new Sustainability‑Linked Financing structures for businesses, including SMEs, in the oil palm and real estate sectors. Firms can earn financing rebates by meeting agreed sustainability performance targets such as greenhouse‑gas reductions or lower building energy intensity. The offers build on CIMB’s RM3 billion SLF programme and its GreenBizReady support. Malaysian SMEs upgrading buildings to green certification or adopting sector‑relevant decarbonisation plans can apply for these loans.

Vietnam’s TPBank secures $50m to expand green finance and SME lending nationwide

October 20, 2026

TPBank signed a $50 million loan with DEG to strengthen its green and SME financing. Sixty percent of the facility will prioritise green projects for emission reduction and energy transition, supporting Vietnam’s climate goals. The bank will also upgrade its Environmental and Social Risk Management System to align with global standards. Vietnamese SMEs can approach TPBank for targeted loans under this new funding line.
