ECHA launches updated SME hub and AI assistant to simplify chemicals compliance

November 11, 2025

The European Chemicals Agency unveiled an updated SME hub with new online tools and an AI-powered virtual assistant to help small businesses meet EU chemicals rules. The resources, presented during the SME Assembly in Copenhagen, bring together guidance from ECHA and Member States in one place. The aim is to reduce the time and cost for SMEs to understand and comply with regulations like REACH and CLP.

Why it matters for SMEs: If you handle chemicals in the EU, this hub gives quick, practical answers that can cut compliance costs and lower the risk of penalties.

Gprnt and PwC release SME Sustainability Barometer, highlight support gaps and fixes

November 12, 2025

Singapore’s ESG data platform Gprnt and PwC launched the inaugural SME Sustainability Barometer surveying 560 SMEs across 19 sectors. The study finds three in four SMEs struggle to start sustainability efforts due to limits in money, skills, and time, and over 70% have not tapped available support schemes. It calls for coordinated public‑private actions and directs SMEs to practical tools that ease reporting and connect them to green procurement and financing opportunities.

Why it matters for SMEs: Use the report’s checklist and Gprnt’s free tools to prioritize actions, meet buyer data requests, and unlock financing sooner.

Read more at Gprnt →

EFRAG update spotlights multi‑language VSME template for non‑listed SME reporting

November 13, 2025

EFRAG’s October Update highlights new resources for small businesses, including a multi‑language digital template supporting the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non‑listed SMEs (VSME). The update also points to recordings and materials from the “VSME in Action” event, offering practical guidance. Together, these resources make it easier for SMEs to structure ESG disclosures requested by banks, investors, and larger customers.

Why it matters for SMEs: Adopting the VSME template lets you answer ESG questionnaires faster and more consistently, improving access to supply chains and finance.
