India’s ADEETIE scheme channels Rs 1,000 crore to MSME energy‑efficiency upgrades with interest subsidies

December 6, 2025

India selected Andhra Pradesh’s Godavari districts to spearhead the ADEETIE energy‑efficiency scheme for micro, small and medium enterprises. The Rs 1,000 crore program earmarks Rs 875 crore for interest subvention, making bank financing cheaper for MSME upgrades, and Rs 50 crore for implementation and capacity building. Authorities also signed MoUs with Energy Efficiency Services Ltd. to scale modern, energy‑saving technologies across MSMEs and public lighting. The scheme prioritises sectors like glass and refractories to accelerate adoption of high‑efficiency equipment.

Why it matters for SMEs: MSMEs can cut energy bills and meet supplier ESG expectations by using concessional loans to replace outdated machinery with efficient alternatives. Contact your state industries department or banking partner to apply under ADEETIE’s interest‑subvention window and seek EESL technical support for project selection and verification.

Thailand’s stock exchange adopts FTSE Russell ESG standards, pushes clean, traceable supply chains

December 4, 2025

The Stock Exchange of Thailand will replace its internal ESG rating with FTSE Russell criteria from next year to align with global standards. SET warned that companies lacking ESG performance risk losing access to global capital and highlighted a new push for “clean and traceable” supply chains. It flagged looming requirements to count and disclose carbon emissions across value chains. SET also pointed to the SET Carbon platform to help SMEs simplify ESG data and reporting.

Why it matters for SMEs: Thai SME suppliers to listed companies will face stricter ESG and traceability demands in procurement. Start mapping supply chains, measure emissions, and use tools like SET Carbon to prepare documentation that buyers will increasingly require for tenders and renewals.

CIMB Singapore and ESGpedia scale sustainability‑linked loans tailored for SME climate targets

December 1, 2025

CIMB Singapore partnered with ESGpedia to expand an SME Sustainability‑Linked Loan/Financing programme. The digital platform helps SMEs set greenhouse‑gas reduction targets and track progress, unlocking preferential interest rates when targets are met, in line with Loan Market Association principles. Over 100 companies have already used the framework to establish credible KPIs and access discounted financing. The model removes costly bespoke verification by giving SMEs a streamlined, self‑directed workflow.

Why it matters for SMEs: Singapore SMEs can reduce borrowing costs by formalising emissions baselines and meeting agreed KPIs. Engage your CIMB relationship manager and onboard to ESGpedia to set targets, document performance, and qualify for rate discounts from year two onward.
