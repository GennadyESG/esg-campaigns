Malaysia regulators launch SME ESG Jumpstart portal integration to speed green financing

November 17, 2025

At the JC3 SME Focus Group Climate Conference in Kuala Lumpur, regulators and banks unveiled a refreshed ESG Jumpstart Portal for SMEs. The programme highlights its new integration with CGC’s imSME platform to offer step‑by‑step guides, self‑assessments, and direct links to green financing. The event’s aim is to provide practical tools, partners, and financing that help smaller firms start or scale sustainability actions quickly.

Why it matters for SMEs: It creates a free, one‑stop route to measure ESG, get help, and access green loans tied to readiness.

EU network spotlights decarbonisation and simpler sustainability compliance for European SMEs

November 17, 2025

The European Commission announced the Enterprise Europe Network Annual Conference (Nov 26–28, Aalborg) with a focus on innovation, decarbonisation and security for SMEs. Sessions will cover how to cut administrative burden, improve data collection and reporting, and help small firms comply with sustainability rules. The event brings EU policymakers and partners together to coordinate practical, scalable support for SMEs.

Why it matters for SMEs: Firms can join to learn concrete ways to simplify ESG reporting and connect with support partners.

Read more at European Commission (DG GROW) →

Free webinar guides small businesses on complying with California SB 253 and SB 261

November 19, 2025

Climate platform Greenly is hosting a session on simplifying SB 253 emissions disclosure and SB 261 climate‑risk reporting. The webinar covers practical steps and templates, including SME/SMB best practices. It aims to move companies beyond minimum compliance toward confident disclosure and long‑term strategy.

Why it matters for SMEs: These laws will cascade through supply chains, so smaller suppliers can use this training to meet data requests and keep contracts.
