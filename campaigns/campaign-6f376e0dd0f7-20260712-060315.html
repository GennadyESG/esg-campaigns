Philippines rolls out free SME tool to prepare SEC sustainability reports

July 7, 2026

A new free “Asia Pacific Green Supply Chain Quick Check” helps SMEs assess readiness for the Philippines SEC’s sustainability reporting. It focuses on supply‑chain documentation, environmental compliance and social‑labor‑governance practices under SEC Memorandum Circular No. 16 (2025). The tool flags gaps and suggests next steps like ISO systems and Scope 3 baselining. Owners can take the assessment now to see what to fix before formal reporting or audits.

Malaysia’s PKSlestari opens ESG assessment and financing support for PMKS

July 9, 2026

SME Corp. Malaysia’s PKSlestari programme offers a self‑assessment with 13 ESG indicators and places firms into five readiness levels. Eligible PMKS can get support such as rebates or financing for solar panels, energy‑efficient equipment, ESG certifications, reporting platforms, greener packaging, and carbon audits. Applications are open until funds are fully committed; completion of the MSME ESG Assessment is required first. The programme’s appointed partner CapBay helps channel financing to participants.

EU anchors voluntary VSME standard to simplify SMEs’ ESG data requests

July 8, 2026

The European Commission adopted a Delegated Regulation creating VSME, a voluntary sustainability reporting standard for non‑listed SMEs. It gives SMEs one common template to answer ESG data requests from banks, investors and large CSRD‑reporting customers. Scrutiny by Parliament and Council is underway; early adoption is possible once published in the Official Journal. SME owners can start mapping their current data to VSME Basic to reduce ad‑hoc questionnaires from buyers.

ESG Playbook launches lower‑cost reporting tiers for small and mid‑sized firms

July 6, 2026

ESG Playbook introduced “Essential Worksheets” and “Premium Modules” to make sustainability reporting more affordable for SMBs. Both tiers run on the same data architecture as the enterprise platform, allowing simple start and later scaling. The offering targets companies that need practical tools without complex, high‑priced software. SMEs can adopt an entry tier now and expand to a fuller system as reporting needs grow.
