HCMC pilot links SMEs’ ESG data to green finance through new partnership

October 28, 2026

At the Autumn Economic Forum on October 27, HCMC C4IR, ICED, Nam A Bank, and SCFC launched a program to help SMEs adopt ESG and reach green finance. The pilot will select 20 SMEs to assess ESG maturity, standardize data and reporting, and build step‑by‑step improvement plans. A shared framework will set the evidence, tools, and criteria banks use to evaluate green lending readiness. After testing, the partners aim to scale support to more than 200 SMEs in Ho Chi Minh City.

Experts outline practical ESG steps and green loan pathways for Vietnamese SMEs

October 27, 2026

At AEF 2026, speakers said many SMEs struggle with ESG because of weak management systems and missing data. Officials advised a staged plan: start with utility bills, add low‑cost sensors on major equipment, then measure product‑level carbon footprints. Nam A Bank noted that firms meeting its environmental and social risk framework can access green loans at preferential rates. Panelists added that strong ESG credentials are now a tie‑breaker in B2B contracts, so reports must be evidence‑based to avoid greenwashing.

SCG’s ESG Company Tour guides SMEs on automation choices and green financing access

October 31, 2026

SCG and partners held the second ESG Company Tour 2026 at Binh Minh Plastics to help SMEs link automation with sustainable operations. Factory demos showed robots, automatic material systems, and real‑time data that improve efficiency and cut resource use. A panel explained how to choose investment items, evaluate ROI, and meet banks’ green credit conditions. The message: build a simple roadmap, improve data, and use these steps to unlock preferential financing.

Ho Chi Minh City coalition launches SME ESG transition and green finance program

October 27, 2026

Four organizations announced a joint program to help SMEs adopt ESG and prepare bank‑ready documentation. The pilot will work with 20 firms to diagnose governance and data gaps, standardize ESG reporting, and define investment priorities. Results will refine the framework before scaling support to more than 200 SMEs. Nam A Bank will advise on data and lending criteria, while C4IR HCMC, ICED, and SCFC coordinate technical assistance and tools.
